The U.S. Department of the Interior (DOI) manages and conserves federal lands and natural resources across America. DOI's bureaus and offices provide REST APIs and data services covering national park information, water resources, earthquake data, mineral resources, geospatial federal land boundaries, and wildlife data. Key API providers include the National Park Service (NPS), the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS), and the Bureau of Land Management (BLM).

**Areas:** Federal Government · National Parks · Federal Lands · Water Resources · Wildlife · Minerals · Geospatial · Geology · Native American Affairs · Public Lands

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Department of Interior: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
